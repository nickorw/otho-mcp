OWL 2 DATATYPE MAP - ALLOWED DATATYPES
========================================
Source: https://www.w3.org/TR/owl2-syntax/#Datatype_Maps

## Core Datatypes (MUST Support)

### String Datatypes
- xsd:string
- xsd:normalizedString
- xsd:token
- xsd:language
- xsd:Name
- xsd:NCName
- xsd:NMTOKEN

### Boolean
- xsd:boolean

### Numeric Datatypes
- xsd:decimal
- xsd:integer
- xsd:nonNegativeInteger
- xsd:nonPositiveInteger
- xsd:positiveInteger
- xsd:negativeInteger
- xsd:long
- xsd:int
- xsd:short
- xsd:byte
- xsd:unsignedLong
- xsd:unsignedInt
- xsd:unsignedShort
- xsd:unsignedByte
- xsd:double
- xsd:float

### Date and Time Datatypes
- xsd:dateTime
- xsd:dateTimeStamp

### Binary Datatypes
- xsd:hexBinary
- xsd:base64Binary

### URI Datatypes
- xsd:anyURI

### XML Datatypes
- rdf:XMLLiteral
- rdf:PlainLiteral

## IMPORTANT: Do NOT use any xsd: datatypes outside this list.
